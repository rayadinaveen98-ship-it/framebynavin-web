# Audience and Commercial Model

Status: **Foundation candidate**

## 1. Primary audience groups

### A. Viewers / fans
People arriving from Instagram, YouTube, shares, search, or recommendations who want to understand who Navin is and explore more work.

Their questions:
- What does he make?
- What are his best videos?
- Where can I follow/watch more?
- What makes his cinema content different?

Primary actions:
- Explore selected work
- Watch on YouTube / Instagram
- Learn about FrameByNavin

### B. Brands and agencies
People assessing creator fit for campaigns.

Their questions:
- What does he create?
- What kind of audience does he reach?
- What performance/proof exists?
- What collaboration formats are available?
- How do I contact him quickly?

Primary actions:
- View proof / media kit
- Understand collaboration formats
- Submit an inquiry

### C. Film / entertainment teams
Producers, distributors, marketing teams, PR teams, indie filmmakers, music teams, OTT/entertainment partners.

Their questions:
- Does this creator fit our film/show/song/campaign?
- Can we work together without compromising editorial trust?
- What promotion formats are available?
- How do we submit or discuss a project?

Primary actions:
- View relevant entertainment work
- Explore film-promotion collaboration options
- Contact / submit campaign brief

### D. Creative collaborators
Filmmakers, editors, designers, writers, developers, creators, photographers, and others who may want to collaborate on future work.

Primary actions:
- Understand Navin's broader creative direction
- Explore projects
- Contact with a collaboration idea

## 2. Commercial philosophy

The website should enable business without turning the public creative identity into an ad catalogue.

Rules:

1. **Editorial and paid work remain distinct.** Payment must never imply a guaranteed positive review or opinion.
2. **Alignment over volume.** Prefer collaborations that fit cinema, entertainment, storytelling, creator tools, filmmaking, technology, or adjacent interests.
3. **No inflated promises.** Viral performance is proof of potential, not a guaranteed campaign outcome.
4. **Flexible pricing initially.** Do not publish a rigid rate card in V1; collect scope, budget, timeline, and deliverables first.
5. **Qualified inquiries beat open-ended DMs.** The site should reduce friction while gathering enough information to judge fit.

## 3. Initial collaboration categories

- Instagram Reel collaboration
- Instagram Story campaign
- YouTube integration
- Short-form video collaboration
- Film / series / trailer / song promotion
- Entertainment campaign collaboration
- Creator / brand partnership
- Custom creative project

These labels are content/configuration, not hard-coded UI architecture.

## 4. Inquiry model

Recommended fields:

- Name
- Company / brand / film / project
- Work email
- Collaboration type
- Platform(s)
- Desired deliverables
- Budget range
- Desired campaign date / timeline
- Project brief
- Optional reference link

V1 budget ranges are configurable and should not imply final pricing.

## 5. Media kit strategy

The website should have a first-class `/media-kit` page that can later generate or mirror a downloadable PDF.

Potential modules:

- Creator positioning
- Current platform metrics
- Selected high-performing content
- Content pillars
- Audience geography/demographics when reliable data is available
- Collaboration formats
- Selected case studies
- Contact CTA

Metrics must include an `asOf` date and should be easy to update.

## 6. Proof strategy

Use two kinds of proof:

### Quantitative
- followers/subscribers
- selected content views
- channel/reel milestones
- campaign metrics when permission exists

### Qualitative
- selected creative work
- process/case-study explanation
- testimonials only when genuine and approved
- recognizable collaboration/press logos only when accurate and permitted

## 7. Conversion hierarchy

Primary commercial CTA:

**Work With Me / Start a Project**

Secondary:

**View Media Kit**

Tertiary:

**Business Contact**

Social-follow buttons should not visually compete with commercial CTAs on business-oriented sections.

## 8. Future revenue layers

Potential future additions, only when they fit the audience and brand:

- long-term sponsorships
- film/OTT entertainment partnerships
- creator/filmmaking affiliate partnerships
- digital resources
- memberships/community
- original products/apps/games/stories
- speaking/events/workshops

V1 architecture should leave room for these without surfacing empty sections.
